About Somaiya Clinical Laboratory

Somaiya Clinical Laboratory is a multi-specialty diagnostic facility functioning under the healthcare division of Somaiya Vidyavihar University. The laboratory is established to offer standardized diagnostic investigations in Clinical Biochemistry, Clinical Pathology, and Hematology for students, faculty, and the public.

The laboratory implements Point-of-Care Testing (POCT) systems to reduce diagnostic turnaround times and ensure reproducible, accurate results adhering strictly to national accreditation norms.

AI-Enabled Urine Test Strip Reader System (KJS-HC-03)

Under the Healthcare Technology initiative (KJS-HC-03), our clinical facility utilizes an automated reflectance colorimeter for urine dipstick analysis. The automated reader reads reagent pads under controlled optical LED illumination, eliminating operator subjectivity and manual color-matching errors.

Diagnostic Functions

The system evaluates key biochemical parameters including urinary glucose, protein (albumin), occult blood, ketone bodies, and urinary pH. The digitized readings are transferred directly into the Laboratory Information System (LIS) database.

Scope of Laboratory Services

Our laboratory provides automated diagnostic screening across multiple clinical departments:

  • Urinalysis & POCT: Automated strip colorimetry and microscopic sediment analysis.
  • Hematology: Complete blood cell counts, hemoglobin estimation, and erythrocyte sedimentation rates.
  • Clinical Biochemistry: Blood glucose estimation, liver panel, renal profiles, and lipid assays.
  • Sample Collection: Sterile blood phlebotomy and urine collection following standard biosafety protocols.
