Patient Testing Process

The standard procedure followed by patients and technicians for routine diagnostic testing:

  1. Patient Registration: Entering demographic details, generating unique Patient ID / UHID, and selecting required investigations.
  2. Sample Collection: Biological sample collection (blood drawing by phlebotomist, clean-catch urine specimen collection in sterile cup).
  3. Laboratory Testing: Automated biochemical testing, photometric strip reflectance reading (KJS-HC-03), and manual microscopic confirmation.
  4. Report Generation: Pathologist verification of test values, reference range comparison, and signing of diagnostic reports.
  5. Doctor Consultation: Handover of diagnostic report to the consulting physician for clinical diagnosis and treatment.

Types of Laboratory Tests

Tests available at Somaiya Clinical Diagnostic Centre:

  • Blood Tests: Routine blood counts, hemoglobin, erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR), and differential counts.
  • Urine Tests: AI-enabled multi-parameter urine test strip examination, specific gravity, and sediment microscopy.
  • Thyroid Profile: Assessment of thyroid endocrine function measuring Total T3, Total T4, and TSH levels.
  • Liver Function Test (LFT): Serum bilirubin fractions, SGOT (AST), SGPT (ALT), alkaline phosphatase, and total proteins.
  • Kidney Function Test (KFT): Serum creatinine, blood urea nitrogen (BUN), uric acid, and glomerular filtration calculation.
  • Lipid Profile: Fasting blood estimation of total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, VLDL, and triglycerides.
  • Diabetes Tests: Fasting blood sugar, post-prandial blood sugar, and glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c).

Description of Common Diagnostic Tests

Detailed explanation of diagnostic terms using standard HTML description list:

CBC
Complete Blood Count: A fundamental blood panel that calculates the number of red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets in whole blood. Used to detect anemia, blood infections, and clotting disorders.
HbA1c
Glycated Hemoglobin: Measures the percentage of blood sugar attached to hemoglobin over the past 8 to 12 weeks. Essential test for monitoring Type 1 and Type 2 Diabetes. Standard reference limit is usually less than 5.7%.
LFT
Liver Function Test: A group of blood tests that check hepatic enzymes including bilirubin, SGPT (ALT), SGOT (AST), and albumin. Useful to diagnose jaundice, hepatitis, and liver cell damage.
KFT
Kidney Function Test: Assesses how well the kidneys filter waste products by measuring serum creatinine and blood urea. Crucial for detecting kidney stones, renal insufficiency, and dehydration.
ECG
Electrocardiogram: A non-invasive diagnostic test that records the electrical signals of the heart to evaluate cardiac rhythm, detect arrhythmias, and identify potential heart muscle damage or heart attacks.
