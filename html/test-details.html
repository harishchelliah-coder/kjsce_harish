Laboratory Diagnostic Test Details Sheet

Official specification document for the Automated Urine Strip Panel (KJS-HC-03) conducted at Somaiya Clinical Diagnostic Laboratory.

Test Name

Comprehensive Urinalysis by Automated Colorimeter Strip Reader
Test Code: URI-003 | Department: Clinical Pathology & POCT

Purpose

The purpose of this diagnostic test is to screen for urinary tract infections (UTI), kidney dysfunction, diabetes mellitus (glycosuria), and metabolic acidosis (ketones) using automated optical reflectance on multi-parameter test strips.

Sample Required

Specimen Type: Fresh midstream urine sample
Volume: Minimum 15 mL to 25 mL
Container: Sterile, dry, wide-mouth container with screw cap.
Note: Specimen should be tested within 1 hour of collection or refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C.

Preparation Instructions

Patients must adhere to the following preparation guidelines to prevent sample contamination:

Clean the external genital area with plain water prior to collection. Allow the first few drops of urine to pass into the toilet, then collect the midstream portion into the sterile cup without touching the rim or inner walls of the container.

Normal Reference Range

  • Urinary Glucose: Negative (< 15 mg/dL)
  • Urinary Protein (Albumin): Negative / Nil (< 10 mg/dL)
  • Occult Blood: Negative
  • Ketone Bodies: Negative (< 5 mg/dL)
  • pH Range: 4.5 to 8.0 (Physiological Average: 6.0)
  • Specific Gravity: 1.005 to 1.030

Report Delivery Time

Same Day Delivery: Available within 2 hours from time of sample submission at the collection counter.
Printed copies can be collected from the registration desk, and electronic reports are uploaded to the patient portal.

Important Instructions

Important Instructions:
Do not take large doses of Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid) 24 hours before the test because high ascorbic acid levels can cause false-negative glucose and blood strip readings. Women should avoid sample collection during menstruation.

Fasting Requirements

Fasting Requirements:
No strict overnight fasting is required for standalone urine testing. However, if this test is booked together with a Fasting Blood Glucose or Lipid Profile, the patient must maintain 10 to 12 hours of overnight fasting (drinking only plain water).

Supervised by: Dr. S. K. Verma, MD (Pathology), Head of Diagnostic Services.
