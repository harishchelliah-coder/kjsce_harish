Test Abbreviations

CBC
Complete Blood Count — measures red cells, white cells, and platelets to check overall health.
HbA1c
Average blood sugar level over the past 2-3 months, used to monitor diabetes.
LFT
Liver Function Test — checks how well the liver is working.
KFT
Kidney Function Test — checks how well the kidneys filter waste from the blood.
ECG
Electrocardiogram — records the electrical activity of the heart.
