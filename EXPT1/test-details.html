Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Purpose: To evaluate overall health and detect a range of disorders such as anaemia, infection, and blood cancers.

Sample Required: 3 ml Venous Blood

Preparation Instructions:
No special preparation is required.
Inform the technician if you are on any blood-thinning medication.

Normal Reference Range: Hemoglobin: 13.5 – 17.5 g/dL (Male), 12.0 – 15.5 g/dL (Female)

Report Delivery Time: Same day, within 6 hours of sample collection

Important: Please carry your doctor's prescription and a valid ID proof at the time of sample collection.

Fasting Requirement: Not required for this test. Fasting is only necessary if combined with a Fasting Blood Sugar test.

Note: Reference ranges may vary slightly depending on age, gender, and lab equipment used.
